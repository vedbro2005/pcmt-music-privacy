Privacy Policy – PCMT Music

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

PCMT Music is developed by PCMT. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Personal Information

PCMT Music does not require account registration or login. The app does not intentionally collect personal details such as your name, phone number, or email address.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your favourites, playlists, and recently played information may be stored locally on your device to provide app features.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services:

  • Supabase: Used to retrieve published music and related metadata.
  • Google Drive API: Used to retrieve and stream music from publicly accessible folders.

When the app communicates with these services, technical information such as IP address and request data may be processed by those service providers according to their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising and Analytics

PCMT Music does not display advertisements and does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services described in this policy as necessary for the app to function.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

PCMT Music does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
